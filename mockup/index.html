Meet Our Founder: Samantha Cooper

As a Florida A&M University Nursing graduate, Navy wife, and mom of two, I know what it's like to put your passions on hold for the people you love.

Volleyball shaped my youth and gave me confidence, friendships, and so much joy. When I looked for a way to get back on the court, I couldn't find a league that felt right - everything was co-ed or ultra-competitive.

So I created the space I wished existed. YaDig Volleyball isn't just about the game - it's about reclaiming joy, building community, and proving it's never too late to return to what you love.

- Samantha
